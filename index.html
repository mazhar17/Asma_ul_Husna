About this selection

This is a selection, not the book’s complete treatment or a list of all 99. Al-Badr discusses the Names as not restricted to a fixed number on p. 58 and the narrated enumeration and meaning of iḥṣāʾ on pp. 62–65. Categories, roots, reflections and related supplications are editorial aids, not quotations from the book.

References & bibliography

Primary book

ʿAbd al-Razzāq ibn ʿAbd al-Muḥsin al-Badr, Fiqh al-Asmā’ al-Ḥusnā (فقه الأسماء الحسنى). Page references follow the supplied 336-page Arabic scan and identify the start of the relevant discussion, not a quotation or full translation.

Further reading

  • Ibn Taymiyyah, Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā.
  • Ibn al-Qayyim, Badā’iʿ al-Fawā’id.
  • Ibn Kathīr, Tafsīr al-Qur’ān al-ʿAẓīm.
  • Al-Saʿdī, Taysīr al-Karīm al-Raḥmān.

Scriptural references

  • The Noble Qur’an, cited by surah and verse.
  • Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī 1314 (ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ, al-Tirmidhī’s grading).
  • Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2713 (the First and the Last).
  • Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2736 (99-Names report, without an enumeration in the narration).

Scope

This is a selected study aid, not a translation of the book. Its roots, reflections, quiz and Qur’anic supplications are supplemental editorial material. It does not replace the book or qualified instruction.

An educational companion. May Allah grant beneficial knowledge and sincerity.
